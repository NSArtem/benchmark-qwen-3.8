As a Game Master, I’ve learned that Old School Renaissance (OSR) dungeons thrive on player agency and mechanical clarity. Here are three core principles to keep in mind:

1. **The Map is the Story**: In OSR, the physical layout *is* the narrative. Avoid walls of text describing the room; instead, let the map speak.
