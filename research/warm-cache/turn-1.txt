# Diagnosis of the "Completed Render Returns to Running" Incident

## Root Cause Analysis

The incident is caused by a **race condition between the API's idempotency handling and the worker's event publication**, compounded by **non-atomic sequence allocation** and **missing idempotency enforcement in the event store**.
