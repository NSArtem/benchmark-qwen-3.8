To find the total number of records, we need to calculate the records from the groups and add the independent records.

1.  **Calculate records from the groups:**
    There are 8 groups, and each group contains 14 records.
    $$ 8 \times 14 = 112 $$

2.  **Add the independent records:**
    There are 3 independent records.
    $$ 112 + 3 = 115 $$

Total records = 115

Final: 115
