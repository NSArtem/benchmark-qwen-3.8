# Diagnosis

The reported incident—completed renders reverting to "running" and duplicate jobs appearing on retry—stems from a fundamental architectural flaw in how the system handles idempotency and state transitions.

**Root Cause 1: Broken Idempotency for Mutations**
The `JobService.create` method checks for an existing idempotency key *before* inserting the job. However, the idempotency record is only inserted *after* the job is inserted and the transaction is committed.
