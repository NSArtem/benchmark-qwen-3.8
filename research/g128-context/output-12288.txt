# Diagnosis

The reported incident—completed renders reverting to "running" and duplicate jobs appearing on retry—stems from a combination of **non-atomic sequence allocation**, **missing idempotency enforcement in the worker**, and **stateful client reducers that do not respect monotonic ordering**.

### Root Causes

1.  **Non-Atomic Sequence Allocation (The "Ghost" Event):**
    In `apps/worker/src/worker.ts`, the `processMessage` function commits the `job.started` event (transitioning the job to `running`) in a separate transaction from the `markSucceeded` call.
