# Diagnosis of the "Completed Render Returns to Running" Incident

## Root Cause Analysis

The incident is caused by a **race condition between the API's job creation/transition and the worker's completion event**, compounded by **non-idempotent state transitions** and **non-atomic sequence allocation**.

### The Critical Interleaving

1. **API Request**: Client sends `POST /v1/jobs` with idempotency key `abc123`.
2. **API Transaction**: 
   - Checks idempotency table → no match
   - Inserts job row with `state = 'queued'
