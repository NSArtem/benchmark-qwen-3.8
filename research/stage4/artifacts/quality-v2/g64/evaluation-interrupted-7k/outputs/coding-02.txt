### 1. The Invariant
**Invariant:** A job in a **terminal state** (such as `succeeded`, `failed`, or `canceled`) is **immutable** with respect to its status. It cannot transition to any non-terminal state (such as `running`, `pending`, or `queued`).

*   **Formal Statement:** $\forall$ Job $J$, if $Status(J) \in \{TerminalStates\}$, then for any subsequent event $E$ where $TargetState(E) \notin \{TerminalStates\}$, the transition $Status(J) \to TargetState(E)$ is **invalid** and must be rejected.

### 2. The Guard
**Guard:** The reducer must check the **current status** of the job before processing any new event.

*   **Guard Condition:** `current_status.is_terminal == true`
*   **Logic:** If the current status is terminal, the reducer must **ignore** any event that attempts to change the status to a non-terminal state.
