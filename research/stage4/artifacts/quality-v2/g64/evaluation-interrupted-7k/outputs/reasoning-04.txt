To find the total number of records, we need to calculate the sum of the records in the groups and the independent records.

1.  **Calculate the records in the groups:**
    There are 7 groups, and each group contains 18 records.
    $$ 7 \times 18 = 126 $$

2.  **Add the independent records:**
    There are 5 independent records.
    $$ 126 + 5 = 131 $$

So, the total number of records is 131.

Final: 131
