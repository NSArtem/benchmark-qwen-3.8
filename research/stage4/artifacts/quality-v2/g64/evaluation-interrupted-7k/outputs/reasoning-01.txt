To determine the number of nonterminal jobs, we need to track the status of the jobs in the queue. "Nonterminal" jobs are those that are currently active (waiting or running) and have not yet finished (completed) or been cancelled.

1.  **Start**: There are 12 jobs in the queue.
2.  **Four complete**: These 4 jobs reach a terminal state (completed). They are no longer nonterminal.
    *   Remaining nonterminal jobs: $12 - 4 = 8$
3.  **Three are cancelled**: These 3 jobs reach a terminal state (cancelled). They are no longer nonterminal.
    *   Remaining nonterminal jobs: $8 - 3 = 5$
4.  **Two new jobs arrive**: These 2 jobs are added to the queue. They are nonterminal (active/waiting).
    *   Total nonterminal jobs: $5 + 2 = 7$

Final: 7
