# Diagnosis of the Lantern Incident

## Root Cause Analysis

The reported incident—completed renders reverting to "running" and duplicate renders appearing—is caused by a **race condition between the API's idempotency check and the worker's completion logic**, compounded by **missing atomicity guarantees** in the current implementation.
