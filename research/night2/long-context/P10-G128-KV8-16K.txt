1.  **Idempotency Race (Duplicate Jobs):** The `JobService.create` method checks for an existing idempotency key *before* inserting the job. In a concurrent scenario (Request A and B with the same key), both requests see no existing row. Both proceed to insert their own job and idempotency row.
